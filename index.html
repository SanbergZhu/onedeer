05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

What minimum order quantity should I expect?+

Each supplier sets its own. Our comparison reports show MOQ and price breaks per supplier.

Can I use my own design or private label?+

Yes. Your information stays confidential. We never share or reuse your designs for other clients.

Do I pay suppliers directly?+

Yes. You pay suppliers directly. ONEDeer invoices its service fees separately.

What if goods fail inspection?+

You see the report before shipment and choose the corrective action that we follow up on. Inspections reduce risk but cannot guarantee zero defects.

Can you work with my freight forwarder?+

Yes. We can coordinate with yours or recommend freight forwarders, and check documents before dispatch.

What does compliance support cover?+

Coordination and document review for EU/EEA, US, UK and Australia, with scope agreed per product and destination. We do not certify products or provide legal advice.
